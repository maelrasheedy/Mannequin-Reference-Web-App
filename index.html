Help

Using the Mannequin

Mannequin controls allow you to rotate, bend, and tilt the 3D model to view it from different angles. After adjusting the mannequin to your desired pose, click the “Search” button to find reference images that match the mannequin’s pose.

Uploading Your Sketch

To upload your own sketch, click the “Upload” button in the right column and select an image from your device. Once uploaded, you can view and edit your sketch before searching for reference images that match the pose in your sketch.

Searching for Reference Images

Once you have adjusted the mannequin or uploaded a sketch, click the “Search” button to find reference images that match your pose.
