Help

Using the Mannequin

Mannequin controls allow you to rotate, bend, and tilt the 3D model's limbs and view it from different angles. After adjusting the mannequin to your desired pose, click the Search button to find reference images that match the mannequin’s pose.

Uploading Your Sketch

Upload function allows you to upload a sketch to improve your figure drawing skills. To upload your own sketch, click the Upload button in the right box and select an image from your device. Once uploaded, the system will detect the pose in your sketch and implement it onto the mannequin. After the figure has your desired pose, click the Search button to find reference images that match your sketch.

Searching for Reference Images

Once you have adjusted the mannequin or uploaded a sketch, click the Search button to find reference images that match your desired pose. Images are sectioned in descending order from closest matches to least accurate matches.

About

For our senior capstone project at the university of Houston - Clear Lake, our team Maquette Three has developed the Mannequin Reference Website to streamline reference image search for artists by providing an interactive browser-based digital workstation designed for instant anatomical and pose reference retrieval.


Currently, the Mannequin Reference Website has ~930 reference images, leading to some limitations in the range of poses available.
